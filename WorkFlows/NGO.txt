NGO registers (uploads documents) → status = PENDING
    ↓
Waits for Admin approval
    ↓
Once approved → NGO creates a Campaign (e.g., "Flood Relief 2026")
    ↓
Campaign stored in MySQL, visible to donors on frontend
    ↓
Donors start donating to this campaign (blockchain transactions happen here — see Donor flow)
    ↓
NGO receives funds in their wallet (MetaMask address linked to NGO account)
    ↓
NGO spends money in real world → uploads receipt/bill (PDF/image)
    ↓
Backend uploads file to IPFS (Pinata) → gets back a CID (content hash)
    ↓
Backend calls smart contract's recordExpenditure(amount, ipfsHash, category)
    ↓
Transaction confirmed on blockchain → tx_hash + CID stored in MySQL too
    ↓
Expenditure now visible to donors with proof attached
